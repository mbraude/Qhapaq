# I-0008-006: Add Future Artifact Workspaces for Approved Consumers

## Objective and coverage

Add a future runtime, integration, website, or maintained tool workspace only
when its consumer, governing requirements, ownership, and delivery scope exist.
No such workspace is selected or authorized now.

## Entry gates

The repository taxonomy and dependent surfaces are established. A separate
approved specification scope, readiness record, exact workspace manifest,
toolchain, and tests exist for the proposed consumer.

## File and ownership manifest

Intentionally pending a real approved consumer. Empty or speculative workspace
scaffolds are excluded.

## Design and integration

Apply the approved taxonomy and policy structure without treating source
language as artifact purpose or introducing private cross-workspace dependencies.

## Tests and validation

Concrete slices must define ecosystem-native restore, format, lint, build,
test, package, conformance, and root-orchestration validation.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] A real approved consumer and governing requirements exist.
- [ ] The workspace is complete enough to satisfy its persistent outcome.
- [ ] Required validation passes.
- [ ] Explicit delivery review covers the exact implemented snapshot.

## Plan approval

Absent. This milestone requires decomposition or promotion to approved slices.

## Delivery evidence and review

No delivery evidence or approval recorded.

# SPEC-0006: Initial Layered Architecture Readiness

## Assessed scope

This assessment covers SPEC-0006 R-0006-001 through R-0006-038 for the
initial .NET scaffold, layer boundaries, public and internal contracts, the
first Service V1 descriptor-retrieval vertical slice, and the associated
architecture, integration, and consumer checks. It corresponds to aggregate
milestone `I-0006-002`; it does not assess all SPEC-0006 requirements.

## Exclusions

Portable-helper semantic ownership and its physical shared-library boundary
(R-0006-039 and R-0006-041), detailed later pipeline-model and execution
capabilities beyond R-0006-038, and their SPEC-0007 work are excluded.
SPEC-0008 work is considered only as a prerequisite to this scope, not assessed
or authorized here.

## Completeness and consistency

SPEC-0006 defines the initial layer responsibilities, adjacent-layer calls,
constructor-injected boundaries, visibility, assembly structure, dependency
injection ownership, first Service V1 use case, security/failure constraints,
architecture checks, and representative model ownership/flow requirements.
SPEC-0007 D-0007-004 and R-0007-404 through R-0007-410 settle the exact
descriptor retrieval Service and CLI behavior. SPEC-0008 owns the separately
gated Entry Point identity migration and source relocation.

No conflicting product semantics were found in the reviewed requirements.
However, the aggregate outcome is not yet decomposed into concrete executable
slices, and its cross-spec delivery prerequisites are not complete. Existing
projects and tests are baseline evidence only, not proof of conformance.

## Conformance and prerequisite artifacts

The scope requires real delivered collaborators and tests, not placeholders.
Its aggregate item explicitly requires concrete child slices, current approved
readiness, completion and delivery approval of I-0006-001, I-0007-001,
I-0007-011, I-0008-008, and I-0008-004, plus their actual output artifacts.

The referenced SPEC-0007 architecture-enforcement and Service/CLI items remain
open milestones without approved exact implementation slices. The SPEC-0008
identity-migration and source-relocation items also remain open milestones
without concrete manifests or delivery evidence. I-0008-008 itself requires
additional policy, conformance, trace-checker, and migration-orchestration
outputs before it can proceed. The existing `conformance-artifacts` readiness
record is blocked; its scope is not a replacement for migration readiness.

Architecture tests, complete DI graph/scope checks, integration behavior, and
clean consumer boundaries must run against actual delivered collaborators.
No promise to create these later satisfies the milestone's entry gates.

## Blockers

- `I-0006-002` is an aggregate milestone, not an executable slice. Concrete
  child slices, exact manifests, tests, and dependency edges must be planned
  and reviewed before implementation.
- Its SPEC-0007 prerequisites `I-0007-001` and `I-0007-011` are still open
  milestones; their own planning/readiness/approval and delivery gates are
  unmet.
- Its SPEC-0008 prerequisites `I-0008-008` and `I-0008-004` are open
  migration milestones with pending exact manifests, scoped readiness,
  migration validation/recovery, and delivery approval.
- `I-0006-001` is not yet complete and delivery-approved.
- Required project structure, architecture enforcement, real Service V1
  behavior, package-consumer evidence, and migration outputs have not been
  produced or validated as a combined initial-architecture outcome.

This readiness assessment is therefore `blocked`; no readiness or plan
approval is granted. Resolve these prerequisites in their owning plans, then
reassess this exact scope against current sources and concrete child slices.
